Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Robust Invest ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application.

Robust Invest is developed by Maverick Roux, based in France. For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us at: robust.invest.pro@gmail.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Robust Invest mobile application available worldwide on iOS and Android platforms.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information Provided by You

To create an account, we collect the following information:

  • Email address
  • Authentication data (password or third-party authentication tokens)

You may also log in using third-party providers such as Apple or Google. In such cases, authentication is handled securely by Firebase Authentication.

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

We collect limited technical data necessary for the operation and improvement of the application:

  • Device information (type, operating system)
  • Crash reports
  • Technical logs
  • Anonymous usage analytics (via Firebase Analytics)

This data is used in aggregated or anonymized form and does not directly identify users.

3. Information We Do NOT Collect

Robust Invest does not collect or request the following data:

  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media files
  • Camera or microphone access
  • Health data
  • Financial or banking information
  • Calendar data
  • Bluetooth or device sensors
  • Cookies or browser tracking technologies

4. Purpose of Data Collection

We use collected data strictly for the following purposes:

  • Account creation and authentication
  • Providing application functionality (e.g., saved favorites, free request limits)
  • Improving app stability and performance
  • Analyzing anonymized usage patterns

We do not use data for advertising, profiling, or selling purposes.

5. Account and Authentication

Accounts can be created using email/password or third-party authentication providers such as Apple or Google. Authentication is managed securely through Firebase Authentication.

6. Payment Information

All payments are handled exclusively through the Apple App Store and Google Play Store in-app purchase systems.

We do not collect, process, or store any payment or credit card information.

7. Data Storage and Security

All data is transmitted using HTTPS encryption. We apply reasonable security measures to protect data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Data is stored using Firebase services.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party service providers:

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Sign-In (if used)
  • Apple Sign-In (if used)

These providers may process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the service.

When a user deletes their account, all associated data is permanently removed immediately. No data is retained after deletion.

10. User Rights

Users have the right to:

  • Access their data
  • Delete their account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent for data processing

Account deletion can be performed directly from the application settings.

11. Children’s Privacy

Robust Invest is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Access to user data is restricted to authorized developers only and is anonymized where applicable.

13. International Users

The application is available worldwide. By using Robust Invest, you consent to the processing of your information in accordance with this policy, including in countries outside your residence.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted within the application or updated in the store listing.
