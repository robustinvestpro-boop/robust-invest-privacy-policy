Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Robust Invest ("we", "us", or "our") collects and handles information when you use our mobile application.

Robust Invest is developed by Maverick Roux, based in France. Contact: robust.invest.pro@gmail.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Robust Invest mobile application available worldwide on iOS and Android.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address
  • Authentication credentials (password or OAuth tokens via Apple or Google)

2.2 Technical Information

  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Crash reports
  • Technical logs
  • Anonymous usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

This data is aggregated or anonymized whenever possible.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos, camera or microphone data
  • Health data
  • Financial or banking information
  • Files or media access
  • Cookies or tracking technologies

4. Purpose of Data Collection

  • User authentication and account management
  • App functionality (favorites, request limits)
  • Performance monitoring and crash analysis

We do not sell or use data for advertising purposes.

5. Account Creation & Authentication

Accounts are created using email/password or third-party authentication providers (Apple, Google) via Firebase Authentication.

6. Subscription & Payments

Payments are handled exclusively by Apple App Store and Google Play In-App Purchase systems. We do not collect or store payment information.

7. Data Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored securely using Firebase services.

8. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to provide the service.

9. Account Deletion

Users can delete their account at any time directly from the application.

  • Go to Settings in the app
  • Click on "Delete Account"

Once the account deletion is confirmed, all associated user data is permanently and immediately deleted from our systems, except where retention is required by law.

10. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Authentication
  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics

11. Children’s Privacy

This application is not intended for children under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Access to data is restricted to authorized developers and is anonymized when possible.

13. International Use

The application is available worldwide. By using Robust Invest, you consent to data processing in accordance with this policy.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Updates will be reflected in the app or store listing.
