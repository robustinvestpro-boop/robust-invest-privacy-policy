Delete Your Robust Invest Account

At Robust Invest, you have full control over your account and personal data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time.

Delete your account from the app

  1. Open Robust Invest.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Your account and all associated personal data will be permanently deleted immediately after confirmation, except where retention is required by applicable law.

Delete your account without access to the app

If you no longer have access to the application, you can request account deletion by contacting us at:

robust.invest.pro@gmail.com

Please include the email address associated with your Robust Invest account so we can identify your account.

What data is deleted?

  • Your user account.
  • Your authentication information.
  • Your personal data stored by Robust Invest.
Important:
Certain information may be retained only if required by applicable laws or legal obligations.
